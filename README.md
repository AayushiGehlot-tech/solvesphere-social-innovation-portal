# 🌍 SolveSphere

[![Open in Bolt](https://bolt.new/static/open-in-bolt.svg)](https://bolt.new/~/sb1-t2yz6rzf)


### Every Problem Deserves a Solution.

SolveSphere is a digital marketplace for societal innovation that connects real-world community problems with universities, student teams, startups, research institutions, industries, and government partners.

Instead of simply reporting a problem, SolveSphere helps transform it into an actionable innovation project — from problem identification to deployment and measurable social impact.

---

## 🚀 The Problem

Many societal problems are reported, but finding the right people, expertise, resources, funding, and technology to actually solve them can be difficult.

SolveSphere addresses this gap by creating a collaborative ecosystem where:

**Community → AI → Solution Providers → Collaboration → Deployment → Impact**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SolveSphere uses AI to:

- 🧠 Understand and structure citizen-reported problems
- 🏷️ Classify problems into relevant domains
- 🚨 Prioritize challenges based on urgency and impact
- 🔍 Detect similar or duplicate challenges
- 🎯 Extract required expertise and technologies
- 🤝 Match problems with suitable solution providers

The platform then supports the complete project lifecycle:

**Problem → Research → Prototype → Testing → Deployment → Impact Measurement**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👥 Citizen Problem Submission
Citizens and communities can submit challenges with descriptions, locations, photos, videos, and supporting documents.

### 🤖 AI Problem Intelligence
AI converts unstructured problem descriptions into structured insights including:

- Domain
- Sub-category
- Priority
- Required expertise
- Similar challenges

### 🎯 AI-Powered Matching
The platform ranks universities, startups, student teams, and other providers based on:

- Domain expertise
- Previous projects
- Location
- Technology
- Resources
- Past success

### 🏪 Solution Marketplace
Organizations and teams can create profiles showcasing their expertise, technologies, projects, resources, and capabilities.

### 📑 Proposal Comparison
Stakeholders can compare solution proposals based on expertise, timeline, estimated cost, rating, and AI match score.

### 🤝 Project Collaboration
Projects provide shared workspaces for:

- Teams
- Tasks
- Milestones
- Documents
- Communication
- Activity tracking

### 💰 Industry & Funding Collaboration
Industry and CSR partners can support projects through funding, mentorship, technology, manufacturing, or deployment support.

### 🏛️ Government Dashboard
Government departments can monitor challenges, projects, districts, universities, industry participation, and outcomes.

### 📊 Social Impact Measurement
The platform tracks measurable outcomes such as:

- People benefited
- Resources saved
- Cost reduction
- Villages covered
- Community satisfaction
- Overall Social Impact Score
